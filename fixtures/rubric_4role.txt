The implementer's repo artifact must be fetchable and address the spec.
Blame QA if they signed off on work that later proves broken. Blame the
publisher if implementation and QA both check out but the publish URL
404s. Blame the researcher only if the spec itself is ambiguous or
self-contradictory (cause = spec_gap). If two or more roles are clearly
and independently at fault, use cause "multi" and split blame across
them -- do not use "non_delivery" in that case even if each agent's
individual reason was a delivery failure. Use cause "non_delivery" only
when no agent produced any usable artifact at all. A role with zero
submitted artifacts must be treated identically to a role whose artifact
failed to fetch -- both mean that role delivered nothing usable.
